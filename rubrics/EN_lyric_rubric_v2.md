## ⛓️ SCOPE CONTRACT (binding) `[中] 純文字鐵律`
Judge sees **ONLY the lyric TEXT**. Melody-dependent judgments — real pitch, note duration, downbeat/pocket, true syncopation/swing/groove, timbre, singability-on-actual-notes — are **OUT OF SCOPE**, must **neither raise nor lower** the score; where a text-proxy exists, score the proxy and disclaim the rest. English spelling isn't phonemic → **all rhyme/phoneme/syllable reads are approximate; label them so.**

## Grounding `[中] 靠山(全文+可信度見 research_dossier.md)`
Pattison, Sheila Davis, Sondheim (*Finishing the Hat*: Content Dictates Form · Less Is More · God Is in the Details — in service of **CLARITY**), Bradley *Book of Rhymes* + Edwards *How to Rap*, Frith/Eckstein (lyric = performed rhetoric/persona), Eliot (objective correlative = **precision not count**), Empson (productive ambiguity), I.A. Richards (tenor/vehicle), Pound; awards GRAMMY/Ivor Novello/ISC; MIR/NLP Hirjee-Brown, Adams, Ohriner, Kim et al., SemEval-2015 (empirical basis to retire sentiment lexicons).

## Scoring philosophy `[中] 制度`
- **Holistic-primary, dimensions-inform**: the 6 dimensions are structured EVIDENCE; final **Craft** = a craft-peer holistic verdict quoting exact lines (CC-10). If one piece of evidence serves several dimensions, cite it once under its **primary** dimension and note the overlap.
- ⭐ **FUNCTION-BEFORE-FEATURE rule** `[家 Codex]`: **no craft feature is inherently good or bad in isolation.** Rhyme, density, simplicity, ambiguity, repetition, profanity, cliché, plain diction, fragmentation, or ornament must be judged by whether it advances the lyric's **speech-act** — who speaks, to whom, under what pressure, in what genre, toward what turn. Reward a "plain" line if it performs the moment exactly; penalize a "clever" line that only advertises cleverness. When dimensions conflict, prefer the reading that best explains the intended **dramatic function**. *(This is the rubric's anti-gaming spine: more rhyme/imagery/wit/ambiguity ≠ better songwriting.)*
- **Bounded override**: holistic may adjust off the dimension pattern by **max ±1.5**, ONLY with a 3-part **OVERRIDE WARRANT**: ①which dimension pattern misleads ②exact quoted line(s) proving it ③genre-calibrated reason. 4 valid warrant types: integrated-excellence / generic-competence / singular-breakthrough / fatal-failure. **The D1 comprehension hard-cap (≤5) applies to the FINAL Craft score and supersedes/nullifies the ±1.5 override** `[家 Qwen]` — an incomprehensible lyric can never be pushed above 5.
- **DIMENSIONAL COHERENCE** `[家 ERNIE]`: craft is an ecosystem, not parallel feeds. If excellence in one dimension **actively undermines** another (a killer D5 voice that renders D1 unrecoverable; D3 rhyme density that forces D6 into filler), that is a **coherence failure**, not a high/low split — name it in the warrant and suppress the dominant dimension accordingly.
- **Dual score SEPARATED**: **Craft** (headline, intrinsic) + **Reach** (hook/market, genre-weighted, LOW-authority) — never averaged. Diagnostic, not absolute truth; final validity = audience/market.

## 🚦 PRE-READ 0 — GENRE LOCK (not scored) `[中] 先辨曲種+信心值`
Cite **≥3 distinct textual markers from different categories** (diction / rhyme-placement / line-structure / subject / form). Output **Confidence: HIGH** (4+ markers) / **MED** (3) / **LOW** (≤2 or contradictory). **If MED/LOW → evaluate against the top-2 plausible modes and report both; never force one genre.** Markers: rap(dense internal+multisyllabic rhyme, off-grid syntax, braggadocio/signifying/ad-libs) / pop(verse-chorus+title-hook, front-loaded hook) / country(title-as-hook, plain diction, concrete place nouns, closing twist) / folk(narrative, AAA/strophic, **sparse rhyme by design**, slow build) / R&B(repetition/melisma, intimacy) / theater(character voice, "I want", want+obstacle, actable) / comedic-satirical(persona/irony foregrounded, **surface ≠ intent**). **GUARD**: on MED/LOW, don't penalize a missing genre feature unless the lyric clearly claims that genre.
- **INTENTIONAL-TRANSGRESSION flag** `[家 ERNIE]`: if a lyric conspicuously violates its own genre markers (a rap with no internal rhyme, a folk song with maximal rhyme), flag **"POSSIBLE INTENTIONAL TRANSGRESSION"** — score the deviation under the relevant dimension but do NOT penalize it as error if the text gives an internal/thematic justification for the break. *(Don't mistake audacity for incompetence.)*

## 🎭 PRE-READ 1 — PERSONA LOCK (not scored) `[中] 鎖說話者+反諷,要證據`
State **Speaker / Addressee / Mode** (confession·boast·plea·taunt·indictment) / **Sincere · Ironic · Satirical · Unreliable?** → **Confidence H/M/L**. If LOW → score the literal surface, flag "IRONY UNCERTAIN", don't propagate a shaky read.
- ⭐ **PERSONA EVIDENCE GATE** `[家 DeepSeek]`: if the declared Mode is Ironic/Satirical/Unreliable, you **MUST quote the exact textual signal(s)** triggering it — incongruity, exaggeration, register-drop, authorial wink, framing contradiction. **Without at least one explicit textual signal, fall back to a sincere reading.** *(Prevents "irony inflation" — projecting sophistication onto plain text; echoes Empson: ambiguity is deliberate only when the text leaves hooks for both readings.)*
- **IRON RULE**: a lyric that sounds hateful may be an *indictment* of hate — judge the craft of the irony, never attribute the persona's stance to the song's value (Newman "Short People").

# THE 6 CRAFT DIMENSIONS (0–10, genre-relative; feed the holistic Craft verdict)

### D1 · CLARITY & COMMUNICATIVE CONTROL `[中] 清晰=分級維度,只在真看不懂才封頂`
On a first, single read, can the listener track **(a) who speaks (b) to whom (c) the active situation/desire/conflict (d) why the key turn/hook matters**? Local ambiguity/density/wordplay/delayed revelation is **fine if the through-line resolves** (Empson richness → score wit under D6). Penalize only confusion that **blocks the speech-act**. Genre-calibrated (dense rap tolerates higher load; folk chorus must resolve simply).
- ⭐ **Operational hard-cap threshold** `[家 DeepSeek]`: the **Craft ≤5 hard-cap fires ONLY if** a well-disposed native speaker, after **two careful silent reads, cannot produce a single coherent paraphrase** of the lyric's situation/desire/conflict/resolution — quote the obscuring passage and demonstrate the paraphrase failure. If **even one reasonable parse emerges, do NOT cap** — penalize only within this dimension. *(Protects dense-but-intentional work like Aesop Rock; fails only genuinely incoherent text.)*
- **Anchors** — Low: pronouns with no referents, syntax inverted only to rhyme, section unparaphrasable. Mid: one locally opaque line but the point lands. High: immediately lucid on first pass while carrying a second meaning.

### D2 · FORM & DEVELOPMENT `[中] 先認出實際曲式再評,別套 pop 模板`
Identify the **actual form** (AAA/strophic, AABA, verse-chorus, through-composed, 12-bar blues, ABAC, chorus-less rap) from repetition — never assume pop. Judge **development not restatement** (situation→turn→resolution vs same altitude), **the turn/volta** (bridge/final-verse reframe that recontextualizes), **title/hook placement** at the argument's peak, **form execution**. **GUARD**: never dock a lyric for lacking a section its form doesn't call for. • **Anchors** — Low: three verses restating one feeling. Mid: clear verse→chorus with a functional hook. High: a bridge that makes you re-read everything before it.

### D3 · PROSODY, RHYME & SOUND (text-only) `[中] 詞的音樂性(合併結構形狀+押韻+flow+音韻織體)`
**(a) Text-prosody** (demonstrable only): line-count signal (even=resolved/odd=forward, vs intent), line-length/syllable symmetry (jagged=filler), end-stopped vs enjambed, natural-stress-as-syntax (word order wrenched only to scan/rhyme?), open-vs-clipped vowel weight vs mood. **Implied rhythmic cadence** `[家 Gemini]`: reward textual architecture (varied phrase lengths, strategic pauses, inherent stress patterns) that builds a purposeful cadence/momentum — *[OUT: actual meter/downbeat/syncopation].*
**(b) Flow-from-text** (conditional): IF line/bar breaks exist → judge **alignment** (clause-boundary-at-line-end = square; mid-line starts+spillover = sophisticated), **enjambment ratio**, **syllabic-density variance**. **IF no line breaks → "FLOW: NOT SCORED (un-lineated)" + down-weight prosody-dependent judgments.** Never fake-score pocket/syncopation.
**(c) Rhyme craft**: classify type (perfect/family/additive/subtractive/assonance/consonance) & placement; score **closure-FIT** not perfect-rhyme density; **rhyme-as-spotlight** (are the rhymed words the important ones?); flag cliché pairs (love/above, fire/desire, heart/apart) + monotone chime + forced rhyme. **Hip-hop (genre=rap) — ORDINAL anchors, NOT fake numeric**: *Low*=end-rhyme couplets/single-syllable/predictable; *Mid*=recurring internal rhyme, some multisyllabic/slant control, scheme supports meaning; *High*=sustained internal/cross-line web, multisyllabic/mosaic, scheme evolves, punchlines land without distorting syntax. **CAP D3≤6** if rhyme density obscures meaning/forces filler (CC-9). "Estimate rhyme qualitatively, label approximate."
**(d) Sonic texture**: alliteration/assonance/consonance **reinforcing meaning** (soft/tender, plosive/aggressive) — reinforcement, not ornament counts.
**(e) METRIC INTEGRITY GATE** `[家 Nemotron]`: if the lyric **declares** a meter/form with baked-in meter (sonnet, ballad stanza, 12-bar blues, "double-time flow"), verify the **textual scansion matches the claim**; a line breaking the declared pattern **without expressive justification** (caesura, syncopated enjambment, deliberate stumble) **caps D3≤6**. *(Prosody is a promise; breaking it unknowingly is failure, knowingly is craft.)*

### D4 · IMAGERY & EMOTIONAL EMBODIMENT `[中] ⭐核心:意象+情感 3 節點決策樹(取代 NRC-VAD)`
**PROHIBITED**: summing per-word valence/arousal (NRC-VAD/any lexicon) — invalid on ironic/figurative text (SemEval-2015). Run the **3-node tree**, prose before number:
1. **Shown-vs-Told audit**: quote the single most concrete, multi-sensory line carrying the core emotion. Reward **hard senses** (organic: racing heart/chills; kinesthetic: falling/trapped) over sight-only+abstraction. **If no concrete carrier → D4 ≤4.** Flag stock images (roses/tears/rain/broken-heart/fire) with no fresh angle.
2. **Correlative precision** (Eliot adequacy, NOT count): do the objects *alone* summon the emotion — one precise image beats ten. Reward superposition over "X is like Y". ⭐ **Metaphor consistency** `[家 Qwen]` (I.A. Richards tenor/vehicle): reward extended conceits whose physical logic holds across the section; **penalize "metaphor collision"** (e.g. *"burning bridges while sailing through the storm"*) unless the genre explicitly licenses semantic dissonance — one sustained conceit outscores three clashing ones.
3. **Trajectory check**: first-line vs last-line — what *changed* (realization/reversal/escalation/decision/**none**)? Quote the turn line. Static wallpaper scores low; earned movement high.
*(Persona/irony pre-locked from PRE-READ 1; score the intended effect.)* • **Anchors** — Low: "you broke my heart, I cry all night" (told/stock/static). Mid: "your toothbrush still bends the way you left it" (shown/organic/precise). High: a concrete image that also enacts the emotional turn in one line.

### D5 · VOICE, DICTION & ORIGINALITY `[中] 聲音一致+用詞+新鮮度+抓罐頭腔`
**Persona-voice coherence** (VVTS consistent — Davis); **POV** identified, unmarked drift=error but signposted shift=reward; **unreliable narrator/dramatic irony**=advanced craft. **Register consistency** + **speaker plausibility** (fits the implied speaker, not "poet ventriloquizing"). **Originality** (ISC first-class): convention can score 7–8, but **9–10 needs a fresh angle/individuation** (kept inside D5/D6, not split out `[家 DeepSeek]`). ⚠️ **"AI-flat" test**: a line is **generic (failing)** if it could move unchanged into a hundred other songs without changing speaker/situation/place/relationship; **plain-but-apt (not penalized)** if simple words reveal a specific speaker/situation (Frith speech-act). • **Anchors** — Generic-fail: "I'm lost in the pain, drowning in the rain". Plain-but-apt: "Mama kept the porch light on till two". High: a voice identifiable blind after 8 bars.

### D6 · WIT & ECONOMY `[中] 機智/雙關/punchline + 壓縮`
**Wit/wordplay**: puns, double entendre, reframed idioms, signifying, turn/reversal, punchline **setup→payoff** density; **productive ambiguity vs muddle** (enriching vs confusing; clarity in D1 tie-breaks). ⭐ **ANTI-SOPHISTRY guard** `[家 Grok]`: **downgrade** ambiguity/wordplay/persona that serves to **evade committing** to a stance or emotional consequence rather than genuinely complicating it — productive ambiguity (Empson) deepens responsibility; defensive ambiguity protects the writer from it. Quote the evasion. **Economy** (Sondheim/Pound): meaning per word — of each word ask "cut it, is anything lost?"; flag padding ("well/you know/oh baby"), redundant modifiers, empty intensifiers, scan/rhyme-filler; reward lines doing **multiple jobs**. ⭐ **"High economy ≠ few words; it means no inert words"** `[家 Codex]` — rap/theater/comedy/character songs may be verbose yet economical if every beat performs pressure, voice, setup, or turn. **Genre-calibrated**: don't penalize apt plainness. • **Anchors** — Low: filler-padded, no wordplay. Mid: one clean idiom-flip or tight compressed couplet. High: a line carrying a pun + advancing narrative + landing an image at once.

## 📣 REACH (separate; informs Reach score only — NOT Craft) `[中] 爆款面,低權威`
Hook/title memorability+self-containment; arresting specific **first line**; singalong/quotability; short-form cuttable moment. **Heavy for pop/short-form, light for folk-narrative/theater** — never hard-penalize a deliberate slow build. **[LOW AUTHORITY — indicative only].**

## 🏛️ HOLISTIC CRAFT VERDICT `[中] 合議庭裁決:先散文後數字`
Prose FIRST: genre call(+confidence) → persona call(+confidence, +irony evidence if claimed) → the 6 dimension findings (each with **exact quoted line** + score) → then numbers. **Craft = holistic peer verdict**; departures from the dimension pattern need the **3-part OVERRIDE WARRANT** (≤±1.5, subordinate to the D1 hard-cap). Ask explicitly: **does this lyric have a point of view / a reason to exist?** **Reach** scored separately. Tail: `【Lyric Total】X.X` (also 中文【詞總分】). Footer: 四模型三輪對抗定版 + 免責兩行(沿用中文版鐵則).
